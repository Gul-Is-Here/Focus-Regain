Delete your Regain Focus account

Regain Focus (package com.focuseregain.app)

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Regain Focus and sign in.
  2. Go to the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account, then confirm.

Your account and its data are deleted right away. If the app asks you to log in again, do so and tap Delete account once more. Firebase asks for this when you last signed in a while ago.

Option 2: by email, without the app

Email gulfarazahmed08@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Regain Focus account". If you used Google or Apple to sign in, use that account's email.

We'll confirm by email and delete your account and data within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your login: email, name, account ID and sign-in method.
  • Your reminder settings, time zone and the date you last opened the app.
  • Your focus session times.
  • Your registered devices and the log of reminders sent to you.

We don't keep any of this after deletion. Google may take a short time to remove deleted data from its systems, as described in Firebase privacy and security.

What stays on your phone

Screen-time history, blocking rules and app settings were never stored with your account. They stay on your phone until you uninstall Regain Focus or clear its storage in Android settings.
